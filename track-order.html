How To Store & Savor Your Sweets

Kaju & Dry Fruit Sweets

Shelf Life: 20-30 Days

Store at ambient room temperature in a cool, dry place. Do not refrigerate to preserve soft fudgy texture.

Pure Ghee Ladoos

Shelf Life: 15-20 Days

Keep away from direct sunlight. Warm in microwave for 8-10 seconds before eating for molten ghee bliss.

Spongy Syrupy Sweets

Shelf Life: 7-10 Days

Refrigerate immediately upon unboxing. Serve chilled (Rasgullas) or lightly warmed (Gulab Jamuns).

Crispy Namkeens & Sev

Shelf Life: 60 Days

Transfer to airtight steel or glass jars after cutting vacuum pouch to keep crispiness intact.

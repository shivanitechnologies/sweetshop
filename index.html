The Secrets to Our Purity & Taste

Every bite reflects half a century of culinary discipline, uncompromising ingredients, and authentic techniques.

100% Pure Desi Ghee

We source certified A2 cow ghee churned by traditional bilona methods. No palm oil, no artificial vanaspati.

Zero Chemical Preservatives

Pure taste as nature intended. Natural sweetness from raw cane sugar, organic dates, and Kashmiri saffron.

Handmade Daily in Small Batches

Slow-cooked in heavy traditional brass kadhais by master halwais with over 25 years of ancestral experience.

Aroma-Lock Fresh Packaging

Food-grade nitrogen sealed vacuum boxes ensure crispy namkeens stay crunchy and syrup sweets stay succulent.

Our Humble Heritage

Honoring India's 5,000-Year Sweet Making Legacy

SweetBliss started in 1974 from a quaint brass-lit storefront in the historic market square, driven by a simple sacred belief: Mithai is not just food, it is an auspicious blessing offered to deities and shared to celebrate life's most precious milestones.

From hand-rolling each Kaju Katli to slow simmering milk for 6 hours for authentic Rabri, our artisans preserve the sacred integrity of Indian mithai craft without shortcuts.

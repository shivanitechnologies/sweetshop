Pure Craftsmanship

The Farm-to-Kadhai Journey

Every piece of mithai and bowl of namkeen honors unhurried methods passed down through three generations of master halwais.

01

Bilona A2 Desi Ghee

Cultured curd churned with bi-directional wooden bilona to separate pure butter, then simmered slowly over firewood.

02

Hand-Ground Spices

Green cardamom seeds, Ceylon cinnamon, Kashmiri saffron, and Malabar black pepper freshly crushed before each batch.

03

Slow Brass Kadhai Roasting

Heavy bell-metal and brass vessels distribute gentle heat evenly, ensuring zero scorching and deep caramelization.

04

Nitrogen Aroma-Lock

Packaged within 4 hours of preparation using food-grade nitrogen barrier trays to seal in freshness without chemicals.

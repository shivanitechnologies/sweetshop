Our Humble Beginning

From a Quaint Brass-Lit Shop to India's Treasured Gifting Destination

Founded in 1974 by Pandit Ramnath Sharma, SweetBliss was founded on one unbending principle: "Never compromise on the purity of ingredients." While modern industrial confectionery resorted to artificial essences, palm oils, and mechanical shortcuts, Panditji insisted on slow-churned A2 cow bilona ghee, mountain saffron from Pampore, and Goan jumbo cashews.

Today, that sacred passion continues under his grandchildren. SweetBliss blends ancestral culinary discipline with modern state-of-the-art cleanroom kitchens and food-grade nitrogen fresh-sealing, delivering royal treats across India.

Our Mission

To preserve and elevate authentic Indian culinary confectionery by providing consumers unadulterated, preservative-free traditional sweets and crunchy namkeens made exclusively with 100% pure desi ghee, premium dry fruits, and sacred craftsmanship.

Our Vision

To be the world’s most trusted ambassador of royal Indian mithai gifting, bridging nostalgic heritage recipes with contemporary packaging, sustainable processes, and effortless nationwide doorstep delivery.

Purity in Practice

Our Preparation Philosophy

No shortcuts, no artificial food colorings, no industrial substitutes. Just pure ingredients treated with time and reverence.

Direct Farm Dairy

Milk is sourced daily before dawn from grass-fed local dairy cooperatives. It is boiled and condensed in-house into fresh mawa (khoya) and chenna within hours.

Stone-Ground Spices

Green cardamom, mace, nutmeg, and cloves are freshly hand-pounded to protect essential aromatic oils. We never use commercial bottled essences.

FSSAI Certified Cleanroom

HEPA-filtered packing bays, hairnets, contactless gloved packaging, and vacuum sealing ensure each box reaches you in pristine hygienic condition.
